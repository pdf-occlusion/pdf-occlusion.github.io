A whole lecture, occluded in a minute.

PDF Occlusion is an Anki add-on. Open a lecture's slides, press Auto-occlude, and every label, key phrase and table cell worth knowing gets a box. You look them over, then make the cards.

From PDF to cards

Everything happens in the add-on you may already use to occlude by hand. Auto-occlude just does the first draft.

  1. Open the lecture

    In Anki, Tools, PDF Occlusion. Add the notes PDF too if you have one.

  2. Auto-occlude

    Boxes land on the labels, phrases and cells a well-prepared classmate would hide.

  3. Look it over

    Drag, regroup or delete any box. Nothing is saved until you say so.

  4. Create the cards

    They go into your deck, and you review each one on its own slide.

It occludes the way you study

Some people hide every label on an anatomy plate. Some only want the ones the lecturer cared about. Tell it once and every lecture follows.

Checked against real cards

Tested on 150 slides a medical student had already occluded by hand, across anatomy, histology, pathology, pharmacology and more. It found about three quarters of what they hid and grouped most of it the same way.

Your collection stays on your computer

The add-on reads the PDF itself and sends only slide images and their text to be occluded. Nothing is kept afterwards, and your cards are made in your own Anki.

Made by a medical student

PDF Occlusion started as the tool one student used to get through slide-heavy blocks. Auto-occlude is the part they wished it had.
