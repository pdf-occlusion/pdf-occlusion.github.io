an anki add-on that turns lecture pdfs into image occlusion cards.

making cards

Open a lecture's slides in Anki and drag a box over anything you want to learn, or drag over a whole diagram and let Detect box every label in it. Group boxes that belong together, attach the notes PDF, and create every card at once. Each card shows its own slide, with the notes and slides a click away.

auto-occlude

For lecture-heavy weeks, Auto-occlude does the first pass for you. Claude reads each slide and boxes what is worth learning; you look it over, change what you want, and make the cards.

You decide how it occludes: every diagram label or only the ones the lecture is about, key phrases or whole bullets, numbers and doses, bold or red text, tables by cell or row, flowcharts step by step, which slides to skip, and your own instructions in plain words. The add-on remembers your choices for every lecture.

The add-on is free. Your first lecture with Auto-occlude is too, and after that it is $14.99 a month for 60 lectures.
