1. scope

This Privacy Policy describes how PDF Occlusion ("we", "us" or "our") collects, uses and discloses information in connection with this website, the PDF Occlusion add-on and the Auto-occlude service (together, the "Service"). By using the Service you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2. information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in, we receive your email address and an account identifier from the sign-in provider you choose.
  • Submitted content. When you use Auto-occlude, the add-on transmits the text of every page of the PDF, a few page images to identify the document, images of the pages you select and the text found on them, the settings and instructions you choose, and any notes you attach ("Submitted Content"). To make cards, the Service first works out what the document is and what each page teaches; those notes are part of Submitted Content.
  • Usage records. For each use of Auto-occlude we record the document name, the number of pages, the date and the credits consumed.
  • Installation identifiers. Linking the add-on to your account creates a key stored on your device. We retain only a one-way fingerprint of that key. The add-on also sends a one-way fingerprint of your computer's hardware identifier, which we use to limit free pages to one allowance per computer; the identifier itself never leaves your computer.
  • Usage statistics. The add-on reports which of its buttons and features are used, page and card counts, errors, and its own, Anki's and your operating system's versions, tagged with the one-way computer fingerprint described above and, when you are signed in, your account. It does not report page images, file names, card text or anything you type. You can turn this off with the usage_stats setting in the add-on's config. This website records the pages you view, the links and buttons you click, whether signing in and linking worked, and the site that referred you, tagged with a random identifier stored in your browser.
  • Payment information. Payments are processed by a third-party payment processor. We do not receive or store full payment card numbers. We retain the customer and transaction identifiers needed to provide what you purchased.

3. how we use information

We use information to provide, operate and maintain the Service; to understand how the Service is used and improve it; to process transactions and account for credits; to authenticate users and linked installations; to detect and prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents; to communicate with you about your account; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. submitted content

Submitted Content is processed solely to provide the Service to you. It is not retained after your request has been completed (notes about the document are kept only while a run is in progress, at most 6 hours), it is not sold, and it is not used to train machine learning models. You remain responsible for ensuring that you have the right to submit it.

5. disclosure of information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We disclose information only (a) to service providers that host, authenticate, process payments or otherwise perform services on our behalf, subject to obligations of confidentiality; (b) where required by law, regulation, legal process or governmental request; (c) to protect the rights, property or safety of the Service, its users or others; (d) in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets; or (e) with your consent.

6. retention

Account information and usage records are retained for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, they are deleted, except where retention is required by law or by our payment processor.

7. security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. your choices and rights

You may sign out linked installations or delete your account at any time from the account page. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, port, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information. To exercise them, contact us as described below.

9. children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. international users

The Service is operated from the United States. By using it, you consent to the transfer and processing of your information in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those of your country.

11. changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page with a new effective date. Your continued use of the Service after that date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. contact

Questions about this policy may be submitted to pdf-occlusion-support@googlegroups.com.
