the add-on

The add-on runs inside Anki on your computer. It reads your PDFs, draws boxes and makes cards locally. Without Auto-occlude it sends nothing anywhere.

auto-occlude

When you run Auto-occlude, the add-on sends the slides you chose to our server: an image of each slide, the text found on it with its position, the lecture's name, the settings you picked and, if you attached one, the text of the lecture notes. The server passes them to Anthropic's Claude model, which decides where the boxes go, and returns the result to the add-on.

  • We do not store slide images, slide text or notes. They exist on our server only while the request is being handled.
  • Anthropic processes them under its commercial terms, which do not allow using them to train its models.
  • For each run we keep the lecture's name, the number of slides and the amount of model use, so you can see your history and we can count credits.

your account

You sign in with Google through Firebase Authentication. We keep your email address and a Google account identifier. Linking the add-on stores a random key on your computer; we keep only a fingerprint of it, and you can sign out every install from your account page.

payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see your card number. We keep the Stripe customer and subscription identifiers needed to give you the lectures you paid for.

deleting your data

Delete your account from the account page. That removes your account, your linked installs and your history right away, and cancels any plan. Stripe keeps its own payment records as the law requires.
