what you get

Auto-occlude places occlusion boxes on your lecture slides as a first draft. It can miss things or box things you would not have. Check every slide before you create cards.

your material

Only send slides you are allowed to use for your own study. You keep every right to your slides and cards.

credits and plans

  • One run of Auto-occlude on one lecture, up to 150 slides, uses one credit.
  • Plan lectures reset each month and do not carry over. Lecture packs do not expire.
  • A run that fails before any boxes come back can be retried. If a credit was used without a result, report it on the help page.
  • You can cancel a plan at any time. It stays active until the end of the period already paid.

fair use

Auto-occlude is for occluding lecture slides. Using it for anything else, or sharing an account, can end your access.

changes

If these terms or the prices change, we will say so on this site before the change applies to you.
