Questions

What counts as one lecture?

One run of Auto-occlude on one PDF, up to 150 slides. Running it again on the same lecture later uses another credit.

What if I go over 60 in a month?

Lecture packs top up the plan. They are used only after the month's 60 are gone, and whatever is left carries over.

Do I need the plan to occlude by hand?

No. Drawing boxes, grouping, cloze cards and everything else in the add-on stay free. Credits are only for Auto-occlude.

What happens to my slides?

They are sent to the model that places the boxes and are not stored afterwards. Your cards are made in your own Anki collection. The privacy page has the details.

Can I cancel?

Yes, from your account page, at any time. The plan runs to the end of the period you paid for.
